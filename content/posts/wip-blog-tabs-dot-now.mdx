Over this weekend, I just wanted to jam out to some songs on my playlist.
I queued up my playlist and started playing a song.
For the next one, I needed to look up the chords, so I paused and took a minute to pull them up.
This happened for the next few songs, and soon, I lost interest in jamming.

I felt that this friction was really unnecessary.
I strongly believe that a repetitive action on a computer must be automated.
So, I built a tiny extension that automates this!

## Tabs.now

1. First, you log in with Spotify.
2. Then, start playing your music on the Spotify app or


Unlike [my previous extension](/blog/bulk-expense-splitwise/), this one is built for Firefox and Chromium.
Turns out, the effort required for cross-compatibility is minimal when the browser APIs used are also minimal.
